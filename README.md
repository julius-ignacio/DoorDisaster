**Disaster Doors — 3D Disaster Preparedness Game (Unity 6, C#)**

Disaster Doors is an immersive 3D educational game designed to simulate real-world disasters such as earthquakes, fires, and floods. The goal is to teach students proper safety responses through interactive gameplay, realistic environmental hazards, and scenario-based challenges.


**
   Key Features**
- Realistic Disaster Simulations

Earthquake sequences with dynamic shaking, falling objects, and safe-spot mechanics

Fire and flood environments designed to test quick thinking and emergency decision-making

Visual and audio cues to simulate real-life hazard conditions

- Engaging Gameplay Mechanics

Player must take cover, navigate collapsing environments, and find safe exits

Panic meter system that reacts to player decisions

Collectible survival items such as medkits and water to regain health and reduce panic

- Temple-Style World Design

Players enter a mystical temple with three doors: Earthquake, Flood, and Fire

Each door leads to a themed “Trial” challenge

Completing trials rewards players and unlocks the final sequence

- Educational Objectives

Reinforce classroom lessons on disaster preparedness

Encourage quick thinking and proper response strategies

Provide a fun and interactive way for students to learn emergency readiness
